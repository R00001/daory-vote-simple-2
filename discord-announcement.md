## :ballot_box: How to Vote

Voting is simple and takes just a few minutes:

**Step 1 — Connect your wallet**
Connect your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, etc.) that holds your Aurorian NFTs. The site will automatically detect your eligible NFTs from the holder snapshot.

**Step 2 — Select your NFTs**
Choose which of your Aurorian NFTs you want to vote with. **Each NFT = 1 ballot.** The more NFTs you hold, the more voting power you have.

**Step 3 — Pick your candidates**
Browse all candidates with their vision, experience, qualifications, and socials. Select up to:
- **5 Councillors** (Community & Outreach, Finance & Investment, Infrastructure & Development)
- **3 Advisors**

**Step 4 — Sign & Submit**
Review your selections in the confirmation screen, then sign the ballot with your wallet. That's it — your votes are recorded!

## :lock: Wallet Delegation — Keep Your Assets Safe

**This is important.** If your Aurorian NFTs are in a cold wallet, hardware wallet, or a wallet you don't want to connect to websites, you can **delegate your voting power** to a separate wallet.

**Why should you delegate?**
:white_check_mark: Your main Aurorian wallet **never connects** to the voting site
:white_check_mark: Use a burner or hot wallet to vote safely
:white_check_mark: One-time setup, then vote normally
:white_check_mark: It only costs a tiny SOL transfer (~0.001 SOL) to verify ownership

**How delegation works:**
1. Go to the **Delegate Wallet** page
2. Enter your Aurorian holder wallet address + a voting wallet address
3. A unique micro-SOL amount is generated for verification
4. Send that exact amount from your Aurorian wallet to your voting wallet
5. Paste the transaction signature to verify
6. Done! Now connect your voting wallet on the Vote page and cast your ballot

**We strongly recommend using delegation if your NFTs are in a valuable wallet.** It's the safest way to participate.
